# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We welcome constructive participation regardless of experience, identity, background,
location, language, or ability. Project spaces should support respectful technical
collaboration and informed disagreement.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Assume good faith while remaining evidence-led.
- Protect private information and credentials.
- Make room for newcomers and accessibility needs.
- Acknowledge sources, prior work, and contributor ownership.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, credential disclosure, plagiarism, sustained disruption, and bad-faith
misrepresentation are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in repositories, issues, merge or pull requests, releases, and other
spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a non-sensitive concern, contact a maintainer through the relevant repository.
For a confidential conduct concern, do not publish details in an issue; use a private
contact method listed on the maintainer's hosting profile and request a confidential
channel. Report vulnerabilities through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, restrict participation,
or ban repeat or severe offenders. Responses should be proportionate, documented, and
handled without conflicts of interest where practical.

This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
